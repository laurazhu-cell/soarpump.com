Technical buying guide

How to specify a fire pump package without guessing.

A fire pump is a project system, not a horsepower-only purchase. Use this checklist to make the first quotation comparable and to expose missing information early.

Send a project enquiryBack to Soar Pump
The five inputs

What a serious quotation needs.

01

Hydraulic duty

Flow rate, head or pressure, suction condition and duty/standby arrangement. If the project has a curve or schedule, attach it.

02

Driver and power

Electric motor or diesel engine, voltage, phase, frequency, starting method and local electrical constraints.

03

Package scope

Pump, controller, base, valves, gauges, accessories, test arrangement and any required installation interface.

04

Approval path

Project standard, AHJ or authority expectation, and whether UL, FM, NFPA or another model-specific document is required.

05

Commercial route

Destination, quantity, Incoterm, delivery window, local commissioning responsibility and spare-parts expectation.

Document pack

What to request before approval.

A catalogue starts a conversation; it does not prove that the quoted configuration satisfies a project. Request the exact model evidence.

TechnicalDatasheet, performance curve, dimensions, connection details and driver information
QualityInspection or factory test records where required by the purchase scope
ComplianceModel-specific certificate, listing or declaration and its covered variant
ExecutionPackaging, lead time, installation boundary, commissioning plan, warranty and spare-parts terms
